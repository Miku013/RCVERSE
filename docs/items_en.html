RCVERSE Items & Blocks Catalog

A comprehensive guide to all vehicles, transmitters, garage furniture, track gimmicks, and tuning components available in the mod.

RC Vehicles

Micro RC Drift Car

microdrone:rc_car_item

A high-performance drift car equipped with realistic suspension kinematics and steering geometry. Compatible with legendary vehicle bodies such as the Hachiroku.

Usage: Right-click on the ground to deploy. Right-click while holding the transmitter to bind and pilot.
RC Vehicles

FPV Micro Drone

microdrone:drone_item

An acrobatic 4-propeller quadcopter. Supports both self-leveling Angle mode and full aerobatic Acro mode, capable of instant 3D flips and power loops.

Usage: Deploy on the ground and bind with the transmitter. Use Spacebar for lift throttle and W/A/S/D for pitch and roll.
RC Vehicles

RC Battle Tank

microdrone:rc_tank_item

A heavy tracked vehicle with independent caterpillar skid steering and 360° turret traverse. Features genuine cannon firing recoil physics.

Usage: Drive across rugged terrain with dual-tread controls. Hold Right-Click or dedicated key to rotate the turret and fire cannon rounds.
RC Vehicles

RC Propeller Airplane

microdrone:rc_plane_item

A scale model airplane engineered with aerodynamic wing lift equations, functional control surfaces, and realistic stall aerodynamics.

Usage: Accelerate along a runway to take off. Control pitch, roll, and yaw surfaces to navigate gracefully through the skies.
RC Vehicles

RC High-Speed Boat

microdrone:rc_boat_item

A racing boat designed for high-speed water skimming. Features hydrodynamic hull planing, bow lift, and dynamic wake foam spray particles.

Usage: Deploy on water surfaces. Cruise at high speeds across rivers, oceans, and custom water channels.
RC Vehicles

RC Exploration Submarine

microdrone:rc_submarine_item

An underwater probe with full 6-DOF ballast buoyancy control and high-intensity searchlights for deep ocean cave exploration.

Usage: Place in water. Use Spacebar / Shift keys to submerge or surface while monitoring the live depth gauge.
Transmitters & Stations

RC Transmitter (Propo)

microdrone:drone_controller

A 2.4GHz multi-function handheld transmitter. The essential tool for binding and remotely controlling all RC vehicles in real-time.

Usage: Hold in main hand and right-click a vehicle to start driving. Shift + Right-Click to open the Dual-Rate & FPV tuning GUI.
Transmitters & Stations

Decal Workshop Table

microdrone:decal_table

A specialized workstation for vehicle body painting and custom sticker livery design. Includes an interactive 3D decal positioning GUI.

Usage: Right-click to open GUI. Place a vehicle inside to adjust paint colors, stickers, rotation, scale, and placement coordinates.
Transmitters & Stations

RC Workbench

microdrone:rc_workbench

A dedicated crafting bench for assembling vehicle chassis, precision micro components, controllers, and tuning gear.

Usage: Right-click to open the RC crafting menu and combine materials into vehicles and accessories.
Transmitters & Stations

Fast Charging Station

microdrone:drone_charger

A dock station block for rapidly replenishing depleted RC LiPo batteries. Displays live charging status and battery percentages.

Usage: Right-click with a battery to insert. Automatically charges and can be retrieved once fully powered.
Display Furniture

RC Display Rack (4-Tier)

microdrone:rc_rack

A 2-block-tall, 4-tier garage display shelf. Displays vehicles in a neat 3D side profile (90° horizontal alignment).

Usage: Right-click each shelf tier with a vehicle to place. Shift + Right-Click with an empty hand to retrieve.
Display Furniture

RC Showcase (10 Slots)

microdrone:rc_showcase

A large 2x2 glass display cabinet storing up to 10 vehicles in a stylish 25° angled perspective showcase.

Usage: Right-click an empty slot to place a vehicle. Shift + Right-Click with an empty hand to retrieve individually.
Track & Stunts

Road Marker (Start)

microdrone:road_start

The origin block for procedural circuit road generation. Allows configuring 4 surface types (Asphalt, Dirt, Ice, Water Puddle), curvature, track width, and banking angles.

Usage: Place at the track start point. Link to a Finish Marker to instantly render a seamless spline road mesh.
Track & Stunts

Road Marker (Finish)

microdrone:road_end

The destination block for road generation, marked with a distinctive checkered flag icon for easy track layout visibility.

Usage: Place at the track segment endpoint. Right-click to inspect connection status with the Start Marker.
