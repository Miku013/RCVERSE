OVERVIEW

What is RCVERSE?

A complete, authentic reproduction of the adult RC hobby world inside Minecraft. Control palm-sized micro machines using handheld transmitters across land, sea, and air — race friends, execute stunt flips, and build massive custom tracks!

🎮

Transmitter & Live FPV Cockpit

Operate your vehicles in real-time with an in-hand transmitter featuring dual-rate adjustments. Switch instantly to the vehicle's onboard cockpit camera (FPV) for immersive, first-person driving thrills!

⚙️

Realistic Physics & Suspension

Features authentic vehicle physics including independent multi-link suspension travel, inertia-based drift counter-steering, quadcopter aerodynamics, and hydrodynamic water resistance.

🌐

Land, Sea & Air — 6 Major Categories

Cars, drones, tanks, airplanes, boats, and submarines. Every vehicle class has unique controls, specialized tuning parameters, and interactive 3D model animations powered by GeckoLib 4.

Step 1: Bone Hierarchies

Follow Standard Bone Naming

Structure your model in Blockbench using standard bone names:

  • root: Master root bone (Rot Y: 180° for North forward alignment)
  • chassis / body: Main vehicle body (connects to suspension & tilt)
  • wheel_fl / wheel_fr: Front wheels (steers and rolls)
  • wheel_rl / wheel_rr: Rear wheels (drive axle rolling)
  • turret: Tank turret yaw traverse (360° aim tracking)
  • cannon: Cannon pitch elevation & depression
  • recoil: [CRITICAL] Cannon barrel recoil slide animation bone
  • muzzle: Cannon tip origin (projectile spawn & muzzle flash)
  • camera_gunner: Gunner sight camera (syncs with turret rotation)
  • track_l / track_r, sprocket_*, wheels_*: Tracks & road wheels
  • propeller_1..4: High-speed drone & aircraft propeller rotation
Step 2: Export & Deploy

GeckoLib Export & Config Registration

Export directly from Blockbench as a GeckoLib Model (.geo.json) and place the texture in your resource pack:

  • assets/microdrone/geo/<name>.geo.json
  • assets/microdrone/textures/entity/<name>.png

Once placed, your custom model inherits all suspension kinematics, steering, and drift mechanics automatically!

Step 3: Recommended Bone Layout

Blockbench Modeling Blueprint Example

Inspect the actual bone hierarchy of our custom battle tank (pzhtank.geo.json) designed in Blockbench. Multi-bone structures like independent turrets and tracks sync seamlessly with GeckoLib!

Step 4: Spawning In-Game

How to Spawn or Drive Your Custom Car

✨ Method 1: Anvil Renaming (Easiest!)

Place a standard RC Car or RC Tank in an Anvil and rename it to your vehicle's id (e.g. sample_custom_car or sample_custom_tank). When deployed, it transforms immediately into your custom model!

🛠️ Method 2: Livery Workbench

Place an RC Car in the Livery Workbench. Custom configs registered in config/microdrone/cars/ will appear in the variant list for instant 1-click preview and selection.

⚡ Method 3: Summon Command

Directly spawn via chat command:
/summon microdrone:rc_car ~ ~ ~ {Variant:"sample_custom_car"}

Step 3: Install RCVERSE

Download rcverse-1.21.1-1.0.0.jar, drop it into .minecraft/mods, and launch the game!
