DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

Development Roadmap & Milestones

Current progress, completed milestones, and planned future additions.

✅ Completed Milestones Completed
  • ✔ Universal Snapshot Interpolation: 100ms interpolation buffer and dead-reckoning extrapolation for jitter-free, ultra-smooth vehicle replication across all remote clients and spectator cameras (v1.0.11).
  • ✔ Anvil Custom Model Recognition: Renaming items via anvil immediately renders and persists custom 3D models in the Livery Workbench (v1.0.9).
  • ✔ Full Localization Sweep: Complete bilingual support across RC Workbench, Livery Editor, all HUDs, and notifications (v1.0.8).
  • ✔ Road Generator Tool: Spline-interpolated asphalt roads, banked turns, jump kicker ramps, and 360° loops.
  • ✔ Multiplayer Sync Hardening: Elimination of controller cross-talk, remote player interaction lockouts, and camera desyncs.
  • ✔ RC Car Physics Tuning GUI: Toggle between REAL (dynamic grip, weight transfer) and RACE (arcade assist) modes.
  • ✔ Mechanical RC Workbench: Sleek titanium frame GUI with recipe catalog and translucent ghost ingredient overlays.
🛠 In Progress / Polishing In Progress
  • ⚙ Track Feature Expansion: Extended redstone/audio signaling for start gates and lap checkpoint plates.
  • ⚙ Acoustic & Audio Polishing: High-RPM motor pitch curves, realistic tire screeching, and water spray audio refinements.
  • ⚙ Tutorial & Documentation Polish: Crafting guides, motor/battery tier overview videos, and comprehensive starter guides.
💡 Planned Features & Ideas Planned
  • ✦ Tire Customization & Swapping System: Craftable tire types (Drift Resin for low-friction slides, High-Grip Rubber for on-rails racing, Rally Block for off-road dirt, and Spiked Tires for ice) to fine-tune handling characteristics.
  • ✦ Procedural Road Surface Variations: Select surface types in Road Marker GUI (Asphalt, Packed Ice, Dirt/Gravel, Shallow Water) with corresponding friction, water spray, dirt kick-up, and hydroplaning drag.
  • ✦ Track Layout Export & Sharing: Preset export/import system for community-designed circuits and stunt tracks.
  • ✦ Community Custom Vehicle Packs: Expanding default vehicle options and unique gimmick accessories based on player feedback.
  • ✦ Multi-Vehicle Tournament Leaderboard: Automated real-time position tracking and tournament scoreboard integration.
VERSION HISTORY

Full Release Changelog

Chronological history of features, improvements, and patches from initial release to latest version.

v1.0.11 Latest Release
🌐 Universal Snapshot Interpolation (Zero-Jitter Multiplayer Sync & Spectator Cameras)
  • Implemented high-frequency 100ms snapshot buffer with dead-reckoning extrapolation, completely eliminating network visual stutter across other players and static spectator cameras.
  • Horizontally deployed across all 6 vehicle categories: RC Cars, Tanks, Airplanes, Boats, Submarines, and Micro Drones.
  • Decoupled GeckoLib 4 model transform offsets, ensuring flawless visual alignment during high-speed drifts, big air jumps, and 360° vertical loops.
  • Verified rock-solid sync stability in multi-client LAN sessions.
v1.0.9 Decal Anvil Sync
🎨 Custom Model ID Recognition in Livery Editor & Auto Tag Persistence
  • Fixed an issue where vehicles renamed via an anvil (e.g., 86, truck) displayed as default models on the Decal Workbench. Custom models now render immediately in the 3D preview.
  • Applying stickers now automatically stamps the custom Variant tag into the item's NBT data, ensuring permanent model retention.
  • Supported across all 6 vehicle types (Cars, Planes, Boats, Submarines, Tanks, and Drones).
v1.0.8 Complete Localization
🌐 Comprehensive Full Localization Sweep (Workbench, Livery Editor & HUDs)
  • RC Workbench GUI (category tabs, tooltips, display slots, and 27+ recipe catalog items) fully translated.
  • 3D Livery Editor GUI (vehicle-specific titles, slider controls, buttons, body part presets, and guides) fully translated into English.
  • Race Display inspect messages, tank elevation indicator, and camera notifications fully localized.
v1.0.7 Road Tool Localization
🛣 Road Marker Configuration Screen Localization & Polish
  • Migrated all parameters in the road generator screen (Road / Jump / 360° Loop modes, road width, bank angles, segments, control points) to translation keys.
v1.0.6 Tuning UI Localization
🔧 RC Car Physics Tuning Menu Localization
  • Sneak + Right-Click tuning menu (REAL/RACE modes, weight presets, counter-steering angle, drift threshold) localized into English.
v1.0.5 In-Game HUD Localization
📊 Vehicle HUDs, Transmitters & Battery Tooltips Localized
  • Planes, Drones, Tanks, and Cars HUD telemetry overlays, transmitter tooltips, battery range blocks, and pilot dummy nameplates migrated to language files.
v1.0.4 Drone Camera & Dummy Fix
🚁 Drone Piloting Camera & Pilot Dummy Desync Resolution
  • Resolved player position desynchronization between player and dummy on drone landing, ensuring smooth transitions upon exiting flight.
v1.0.3 Multiplayer Safety Patch
🔒 Multiplayer Interaction Lockout & Anti-Griefing Protection
  • Protected active RC vehicles from interference by other players on dedicated and LAN servers, preventing input crossover and unauthorized hijacking.
v1.0.2 Race Display Enhancement
⏱ Race Display Multiblock Synchronization & Lap Timer Accuracy
  • Optimized large multi-panel screen linking, enhanced millisecond lap timing accuracy, and improved live placement displays.
v1.0.1 Workbench GUI v2
🔩 Next-Gen Mechanical RC Workbench with Translucent Ghost Overlays
  • Introduced CNC-milled titanium frame interface with left-hand recipe selector and translucent 3x3 ghost ingredient projection.
v1.0.0 Initial Official Release
🎉 Initial Official Release of RCVERSE
  • Introduced Land, Sea, and Air vehicles: RC Cars (AE86 & more), Micro Drones, RC Tanks, Airplanes, Speedboats, and Submarines.
  • Packed with Transmitter control, 3D Livery Editor, Rapid Charger, Showcase Furniture, and Modular Racing Circuit blocks.
