🏎️ Dynamic Drift Action & Real Physics


Inertia-based counter-steer drifting, independent suspension travel, and live ground dust effects!

RCVERSE brings the authentic adult RC hobby into Minecraft. Hold your handheld transmitter to steer micro-sized machines across land, sea, and air with dedicated physics engines tailored for every vehicle class:

  • 🏎️ RC Cars & Drift Machines: Independent multi-link suspension, throttle braking, and customizable grip/drift multipliers.
  • 🛸 Micro Drones & Quadcopters: Realistic acrobatic flips, pitch/roll/yaw flight physics, and propeller spin.
  • 💥 RC Battle Tanks: 360° turret traverse, cannon elevation, ground dust shockwaves, and animated barrel recoil!
  • ✈️ RC Airplanes: High-speed runway takeoff, stall aerodynamics, and graceful sky glides.
  • 🚤 RC Speedboats: Hydrodynamic water drag and high-speed wake spray.
  • ⚓ RC Submarines: Deep-sea buoyancy diving and exploration.
  • 🚲 RC Bicycles: Dual-wheel gyroscopic balance and handlebar steering.

🌐 Complete Vehicle Lineup


All machines are ready to craft, upgrade, and race with friends!

🏁 Dynamic Stunt Loops & Spline Race Tracks


Place Road Generation Markers to create procedural asphalt tracks, banked corners, and 360° vertical stunt loops!

🎨 Import Custom Blockbench Models (Zero Coding Required!)

Want to drive your own dream car or historical battle tank? RCVERSE allows you to create custom 3D models in Blockbench and load them via standard Resource Packs and JSON configs without recompiling the mod!

  • 📦 Starter Template Kits Available: Download official starter kits for RC Cars and RC Tanks right from our Official Website.
  • ✨ Instant Anvil Renaming: Rename any RC vehicle on an Anvil to your custom model ID, and it instantly transforms in-game!

🛠️ Requirements & Installation

  1. Ensure you are running Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge.
  2. Install GeckoLib 4 (Mandatory dependency for 3D animations).
  3. Drop RCVERSE (.jar) into your .minecraft/mods folder.
  4. Launch Minecraft, grab a Transmitter and an RC vehicle, and have fun!
